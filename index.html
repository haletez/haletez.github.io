Personal game project · In development

Somethings Phishy

The police department’s donut and coffee fund has gone missing. In this 2D side-scroller, players collect evidence and learn how to spot phishing clues.

  • Godot
  • GDScript
How the game works

I’m designing the investigation around an evidence board that helps players connect clues. The final police report doubles as a quiz, so solving the case means putting what they learned into practice.
